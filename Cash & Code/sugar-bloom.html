Our Story

From Our Kitchen
to Your Table

Sugar & Bloom started as a tiny home-kitchen dream in 2019, baking birthday cakes for neighbors and friends. Today, every cake, cookie, and croissant is still made the same way — from scratch, with real butter, real fruit, and a lot of love.

We believe dessert isn't just food. It's a little moment of joy.
