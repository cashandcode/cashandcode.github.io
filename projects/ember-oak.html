Our Story

Cooked over fire,
rooted in craft.

Ember & Oak was born from a simple idea: great food doesn't need to be complicated. Founded in 2018 by Chef Daniel Cross, our kitchen blends wood-fired technique with seasonal, locally-sourced ingredients to create dishes that feel both familiar and new.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen reflects our commitment to craft, community, and comfort — cooked the slow way, over real fire.

Fire is the oldest ingredient we have. I just try not to get in its way.

Chef Daniel Cross

Executive Chef & Founder
